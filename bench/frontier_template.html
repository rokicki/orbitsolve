How these were measured

Solve time excludes loading (gullicube's pools and finish tables; brobdicube's algorithm files), and includes planning, search, merging and output. brobdicube runs with -p ⌈N/(2·threads)⌉ so its phases split across the threads; -g is its greedy planning (default planning is optimal and takes about 10 s at 4³).

gullicube effort levels: --fast (width-1 greedy, no merge), --fast --finish (plus exact one-algorithm finishes), and quality mode at beam widths 1–1024 (finish tables, mined wing pool, seam-aware costs, one merge, merge time budgeted at half the pair search time). A setting is dropped at larger sizes once a run exceeds two minutes.

Moves count one per layer turn, half turns 1. dwalton76 counts outer block turns (a wide turn is one move) and reports moves only, so his line is a reference for length, not a point in time.
